WristSun privacy / 日光时刻隐私

Version 0.1.0 · 2026-10-11

English

WristSun calculates astronomical solar events locally on your watch. It has no account, backend, ads, analytics or location-upload service. Manual coordinates, IANA time zone, date selection and language are stored locally so the app can recover offline.

Location is optional and used only to select coordinates on this watch. You can refuse permission and enter a place manually. The app does not request background location or maintain a location history. Coordinates alone do not determine a time zone: verify the chosen zone yourself.

Remove the app to remove local settings. Your device's own backup and operating-system services are controlled by its vendor. Times are estimates and do not predict cloud cover or actual illumination. See support for usage and limitations.

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its hosting provider may process request information such as IP addresses and server logs; that is separate from the watch app’s local calculation. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

中文

日光时刻在手表本地计算太阳事件，无账号、服务器、广告、分析统计或位置上传服务。手工坐标、IANA 时区、日期选择和语言保存在本地，以便离线恢复。

定位为可选项，仅用于在此手表上选择坐标。拒绝定位后仍可手工输入。本应用不请求后台定位，也不保留位置历史。坐标本身不确定时区，请核对所选时区。

删除应用会删除本地设置。设备自带的备份及系统服务由设备厂商控制。时间为天文估算，不预测云量或实际光照。用法与限制见支持页面。

本支持网站托管于 GitHub Pages。托管方可能处理 IP 地址、访问日志等请求信息，与手表应用的本地计算不同。请参阅GitHub 隐私声明。
