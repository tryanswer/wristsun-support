WristSun support / 日光时刻支持

Choose your place / 选择地点

Open Place & date. Enter latitude −90…90, longitude −180…180, an IANA time zone such as Asia/Shanghai or America/New_York, and YYYY-MM-DD. Save only accepts valid values. Offline presets are sample places, not your detected location. Optional location changes coordinates; verify the time zone.

进入地点与日期，输入有效纬度、经度、IANA 时区及 YYYY-MM-DD 日期。保存前会校验输入。离线预设是示例地点，并非检测到的当前位置。可选定位会更新坐标，请核对时区。

Read times / 阅读时间

The selected zone controls the local calendar day and daylight-saving changes. Sunrise/sunset use solar centre −0.833°. Golden is −4° to +6°, blue −6° to −4°. These are photography conventions. Clouds, terrain, observer altitude and near-polar short crossings affect practical accuracy. No-crossing states are intentional, not missing internet data. Remaining daylight and next event refer to the selected local date.

时间遵循所选时区及夏令时。日出／日落：−0.833°；黄金：−4° 至 +6°；蓝调：−6° 至 −4°。这是摄影定义。云层、地形、观测海拔及极地极短交点会影响实际精度。无事件状态不代表缺少联网数据。剩余日光和下一事件均对应所选的当地日期。

Recover offline / 离线恢复

Place, selected date and language remain on the watch after closing the app. Refusing location does not block manual entry. If a date/zone is invalid or a civil date was skipped, choose another date or zone. Try Today to return to the current day at your selected place.

关闭重开后保留地点、所选日期与语言。拒绝定位不影响手工输入。日期或时区无效、当地日期被跳过时，请更换日期或时区；点击今天可返回所选地点的当前日期。
